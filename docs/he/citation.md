2. Murshudov, G. N., et al. (2011). *REFMAC5 for the refinement of macromolecular crystal structures*. Acta Crystallogr. D Biol. Crystallogr., 67(4), 355–367.
3. Yamashita, K., Palmer, C. M., Burnley, T., & Murshudov, G. N. (2021). *Cryo-EM model refinement using Servalcat*. Acta Crystallogr. D Struct. Biol., 77(10), 1282–1291.
4. Agirre, J., et al. (2023). *The CCP4 suite: integrative software for macromolecular crystallography*. Acta Crystallogr. D Struct. Biol., 79(6), 449–461.
5. Abraham, M. J., et al. (2015). *GROMACS: High performance molecular simulations through multi-level parallelism from laptops to supercomputers*. SoftwareX, 1–2, 19–25.
6. Wojdyr, M. (2022). *GEMMI: a library for structural biology*. J. Appl. Crystallogr., 55(4), 1105–1112.
